About Me

I'm Supreeth Vijay, a UX & Product Designer from Bengaluru — someone who believes design is less about pixels and more about people.

Growing up as a physical challenge taught me resilience, patience, and how thoughtful design can restore independence. From overcoming surgeries to earning "Best Outgoing Student" at AVES in 10th, at PES University in MCA, to working across Wipro, ANZ, Infosys, and Retisio Pvt Ltd, I've lived every phase as a learning curve in problem-solving and empathy.

After six years in tech, I transitioned into UX, blending my engineering roots with human-centred design to craft interfaces that are accessible, emotionally intelligent, and meaningful.

For me, good design doesn't just look elegant, it helps people feel capable.
